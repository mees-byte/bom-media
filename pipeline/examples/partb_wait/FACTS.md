## Confirmed
1. 2027 COLA 3.6%, average retirement benefit +about $75/month, from January payments. — SSA blog 2026-10-14 (see ruf_cola_test FACTS 1–3)
2. 2026 Part B premium $202.90, up $17.90 (9.7%) from $185.00; deductible $283 (from $257). Announced Nov 14, 2025. — CMS fact sheet https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/2026-medicare-parts-b-premiums-deductibles ; Becker's
3. 2026 COLA was 2.8% → premium rose ~3.5x faster than the raise (9.7/2.8). — ruf_cola_test FACTS H1–H3
4. Part B premium usually deducted directly from Social Security. — lowincomerelief.com 2026-11-13; AARP
5. Net ≈ $75 − increase (illustrative, average retiree, standard premium only). With projection: ≈ $68. — medicaldaily.com 2026-11-17
6. Hold harmless: for most people with premium deducted from SS, a Part B increase can't make the net check smaller than last year. Not covered: new to Part B, premium not deducted (Medicare but no SS yet), IRMAA payers (and dual eligibles, whose premium the state pays). — MedPAC Mar 2026; SSA blog 2020-11-30
7. Arithmetic: hold harmless only bites when 3.6% × benefit < increase → benefit < increase/0.036 (projection: ~$183).
8. IRMAA: income-related surcharge on Part B (and Part D); 2026 starts above $109,000 MAGI single / $218,000 joint; based on tax return from 2 years earlier (2026 ← 2024; 2027 ← 2025); next bracket 2026 Part B $284.10. — GoodRx, NerdWallet, countrytaxcalc (CMS/SSA POMS)
9. Form SSA-44: request a new determination after a qualifying life-changing event (8 listed: marriage, divorce/annulment, death of spouse, work stoppage, work reduction, loss of income-producing property, loss of pension income, employer settlement payment). One-time income spikes (home sale, Roth conversion) don't qualify. — michaelryanmoney.com (SSA-44), nationaltaxtools.com (SSA POMS HI 01120.005), ustax.tools
10. Medicare Savings Programs (QMB, SLMB, QI) run by states can pay the Part B premium for people with limited income and resources; apply via state Medicaid office; Medicare.gov: "Even if you don't think you qualify, you should still apply." If enrolled, Part B premium no longer deducted from SS; MSP enrollees automatically get Extra Help (Part D). — https://www.medicare.gov/medicare-savings-programs ; Medicare Interactive ; Medicare.gov pub 12132
11. COLA notice: one page with new benefit, deductions (Medicare), net amount; online in my Social Security late November, mail in December. — SSA blog 2026-10-14
12. Part D 2027: deductible max $700, out-of-pocket cap $2,400. Part D / MA premiums can also be deducted from the check. — Kiplinger; AOL/FinanceBuzz
13. Open enrollment ends Dec 7 (changes to Part D / MA). — Medicare.gov
