# FACTS — "Is the Government Keeping Your Medicare Money? The $90 Check, Explained" (2026-11-12)
1. White House announced one-time $90 Part B payment Oct 2, 2026; CMS published rules Oct 3. — govtschemes.org; seniorssecrets.com
2. Most eligible got it by direct deposit from Social Security on/around Oct 8; others by Treasury check later in October. — budgetseniors.com; moneyinstructor.com (CMS)
3. ~62.9 million enrolled in Part B; 20.8 million eligible (CMS fact sheet). — Dallas News
4. Eligible: Original Medicare Part B, live in U.S., no Medicaid premium help, no IRMAA. Excluded: Medicare Advantage, Medicaid premium help, IRMAA payers. — CMS via United Medicare Advisors / Dallas News / medicarechoose
5. IRMAA affects roughly 8% of people with Part B (CMS). — talktomira.com
6. Money from the Medicare Improvement Fund (~$2 billion); Medicare Rights Center: not a true premium refund. — govtschemes.org (Medicare 2027 costs)
7. $90 ≈ 44% of one month's standard premium. — budgetseniors.com
8. Part B standard premium $185.00 (2025) → $202.90 (2026): +$17.90/mo = $214.80/yr. — CMS via myfederalretirement.com
9. By law Part B premium = 25% of estimated Part B costs for enrollees 65+. — CMS press release (Nov 12, 2021)
10. 2027 premium not yet announced; Trustees project $209.50 (+$6.60); CMS usually announces mid-November (2026 premium announced Nov 14, 2025). — comparemedicareadvantage.org; myfederalretirement.com
11. Medicare Advantage members generally still pay the Part B premium. — Medicare.gov (general rule)
12. Budget Seniors: don't switch plans to chase a one-time $90 — doctors, drugs, costs dwarf $90. — budgetseniors.com
