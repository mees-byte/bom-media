It's official: Medicare's standard Part B premium for 2027 is $209.40 a month, up $6.50 from $202.90. For most retirees it comes straight out of the Social Security check. If you get the average raise of about $75, roughly $68.50 is left after the new premium.

And for the first time since 2023, the Part B premium is growing at a slower rate than the Social Security raise (3.2% vs. 3.6%).
