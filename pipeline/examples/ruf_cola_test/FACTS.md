# FACTS — Retirement Under Fire test video: 2027 COLA (researched 2026-11-09)

1. 2027 COLA = 3.6%, effective with January 2027 payments. — SSA blog 2026-10-14 https://www.ssa.gov/blog/en/posts/2026-10-14.html ; SSA https://www.ssa.gov/news/en/cola
2. Average retirement benefit rises about $75/month. — SSA blog 2026-10-14
3. Nearly 72 million Social Security beneficiaries get the COLA (76 million incl. SSI). — SSA press release https://www.ssa.gov/news/en/mip/2026-10-01-f.html ; SSA blog
4. No application needed; applied automatically. — NewsNation 2026-10 https://www.newsnationnow.com/business/your-money/social-security-cola-2027-3/amp/
5. COLA = increase in CPI-W, Q3 (July–Sept) average vs Q3 of last COLA year; BLS produces CPI-W (Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers). — https://www.ssa.gov/news/en/cola ; Cronista
6. 3.6% is the largest COLA in four years (2024 3.2, 2025 2.5, 2026 2.8; 2023 8.7). — NewsNation COLA list
7. Medicare Part B premium usually deducted from Social Security. — AARP https://www.aarp.org/medicare/medicare-part-b-premium-2027/
8. 2026 standard Part B premium $202.90/month. — CMS via AARP / myfederalretirement.com
9. 2026 Medicare Trustees Report projects 2027 standard Part B premium $209.50 (+$6.60). Official CMS figure comes in November (not announced as of late Oct 2026). — fedratecalc.com, ustax.tools, getsmartquotes.com (as of 2026-10-26)
10. $75 − $6.60 ≈ $68 (illustrative arithmetic, only if projection holds and standard premium).
11. Part D / Medicare Advantage premiums may also be deducted from the check. — AOL/FinanceBuzz COLA explainer
12. Hold-harmless: Part B premium increase cannot reduce prior-year net Social Security payment, for those with premium deducted from SS. Does not apply to: new to Part B, premium not deducted (e.g. Medicare but not yet claiming SS), IRMAA payers, dually eligible. — MedPAC Mar 2026 https://www.medpac.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Tab-H-Part-B-premiums-payment-basics-Mar-26.pdf ; SSA blog 2020-11-30 ; Kitces
13. With a $6.60 increase vs 3.6% COLA, hold harmless only matters for benefits under ~$183/month (6.60/0.036) — arithmetic.
14. COLA notice: simplified one page, shows new benefit amount, deductions (Medicare), and net amount. Online in my Social Security late November; by mail in December. — SSA blog 2026-10-14; Disabled World 2027 COLA; FinanceBuzz
15. Voluntary tax withholding also appears on notice. — FinanceBuzz https://financebuzz.com/social-security-1850-benefit-next-year
