Worked example (own arithmetic on S1 rules, hypothetical person):
- Claims at 63, check $1,400/mo, earns $35,560 in 2027 → $10,000 over $25,560 → $5,000 withheld.
- $5,000 / $1,400 = 3.57 → SSA holds Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr checks = $5,600; the extra $600 is paid in 2028 (method per S3 example).
- Rest of year (May–Dec, 8 checks) paid in full.
