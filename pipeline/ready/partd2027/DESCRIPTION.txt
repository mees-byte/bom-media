TITLE: Medicare Part D 2027: Drug Plan Premiums Could Jump 50% Unless You Switch by December 7

The average premium for stand-alone Medicare drug plans is projected to jump 50% in 2027, from $34 to $51 a month, if people keep the plan they have (KFF). Medicare itself projected an increase of less than $1. Both numbers come from real analyses; they answer different questions. Open enrollment ends December 7.

What you'll learn:
• Why KFF says +50% and Medicare says less than $1
• Why premiums are jumping: the temporary Part D Premium Stabilization Demonstration ends after 2026
• Who feels it: 76% of 13.6 million stand-alone plan enrollees without Extra Help would pay more if they change nothing; no $0-premium stand-alone plans in 2027
• The 2027 deductible ($700) and out-of-pocket cap ($2,400, not counting premiums)
• What happens if you do nothing

Medicare Advantage plans with drug coverage are different: their drug premiums average about $9 a month in 2027.
